Why lecturers use review flags

Start with the flagged sentences

On our test clips DUBBE flagged 3 of 15 and 11 of 42 sentences, so a teaching assistant knows where to start. Flags miss some confident mistakes — a final listen is still needed.

Terms stay right

Correct a technical term once in the editor; your text is never re-translated.

Subtitles too

SRT and WebVTT in both languages for your learning platform, timed to the dubbed video.

Built for course videos

  • Long videos without drift — every sentence is placed where it was said.
  • Slides stay in sync — DUBBE can slow the picture slightly instead of rushing the voice.
  • Your files, not a platform's — download an MP4 for any learning platform (YouTube's free auto-dubbing only works for videos published on YouTube).
  • Privacy — videos are deleted after your plan's retention period, or whenever you delete them. Privacy notice

For schools

Students and staff with a school email address (for example .ac.kr or .edu) will get extra free minutes — the amount is being decided. Departments that want to dub a whole course can contact us.

DUBBE is a student project and non-commercial during the beta: the translation model is licensed for non-commercial use only. Teaching use is fine; selling the dubbed videos is not.
