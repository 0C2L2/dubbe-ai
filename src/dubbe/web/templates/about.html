About

A dub that tells you what to check

Every step of automatic dubbing is a model, and errors compound: a misheard word becomes a confident wrong translation, then fluent synthetic speech that says something wrong. DUBBE's answer is a second output next to the dub: a short list of sentences a person should check, shown on the video's timeline.
