Terms

  1. What DUBBE is. A student prototype that dubs videos with AI. It is provided "as is", may be slow or unavailable, and makes mistakes; check every dub before you use it.
  2. Your videos. Upload only videos you own or have a licence to change. Videos under a No-Derivatives licence are not allowed. You keep all rights to your videos.
  3. Non-commercial use. During the beta, use dubs for non-commercial purposes only (teaching, study, personal use): the translation model is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0.
  4. AI label. Every dub is AI-generated. Do not remove the label or present a dub as a human recording.
  5. Use policy. Follow the Use policy. We may delete dubs and close accounts that break it.
  6. Plans. Minutes and limits are shown on Plans. Nothing is charged during the beta.
  7. Ending. You can delete your account at any time in Settings. We may end the beta; we will tell you first and give you time to download your files.
  8. Contact. Contact form.

Use policy

Do not use DUBBE to:

  • impersonate a person, or make someone appear to say something they did not mean;
  • dub videos of other people without the rights to them;
  • deceive viewers — for example about elections, news, health or money;
  • harass, threaten or defame anyone, or create sexual content involving real people;
  • remove or hide the AI label, or claim a dub is a human recording;
  • try to get around plan limits, or attack the service.

See something that breaks these rules? Report misuse.

  • No tracking or advertising cookies, no analytics, nothing sold.
  • Who processes your data: during the beta everything runs on the team's own computer. If we move the dubbing to a rented GPU (planned: Nosana) or use an email service, we will name it here before your data goes there.
  • Who can see your videos: you, and DUBBE team members when fixing a problem.
  • Your rights (Korean Personal Information Protection Act): see, download and delete your data — delete projects and your account yourself in Settings, or ask us through the contact form (topic "Privacy or data request").
  • Voice clones: only if you choose to clone your own voice. A voice can be biometric data, so we ask a separate consent first. The recording is stored encrypted, used only to make your clone speak, kept until you delete the voice or your account, and never given to anyone. Other users can use your clone only if you share it in the voice library; you can stop sharing at any time. Everything a clone says carries an inaudible watermark (Resemble AI's Perth) and the AI label.
  • Speakers in your video: to tell people apart we compare their voices while the job runs; these voice fingerprints are not stored, only which sentence belongs to which speaker. A speaker's voice is cloned from the video only if you confirm that person agreed. That recording is stored encrypted inside the project, used only to dub that project, never shared or listed, and deleted with the project. In Speaker Detection you can instead save a speaker to your voices, with the same confirmation; it stays private, can never be published, and is deleted with the voice.
