Questions

About plans

What counts as a minute?

The length of each new video you upload.

Do unused minutes carry over?

No, minutes reset on the 1st of each month.

Are re-dubs counted?

No. Correcting sentences and re-dubbing uses no minutes.

What happens at the limit?

The upload is refused with a message saying how many minutes you have left; nothing is charged.

Where do I see my minutes?

In the top bar when signed in, and on Plan & usage.

How do I cancel or delete my data?

There is nothing to cancel on Free. Delete a project, or your whole account in Settings; the files are removed at once.
