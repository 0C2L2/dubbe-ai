Questions

Does it keep the original speaker's voice?

Not by default: DUBBE picks a preset voice close to each speaker's pitch. If you confirm a speaker agreed, the editor can clone them from the video for that project. You can also choose another preset, or a clone of your own voice.

Can I upload only audio?

Not yet: upload a video (MP4 or MOV), or paste a link to one.

Do I get subtitles?

Yes: SRT and WebVTT in {{ a }} and in {{ b }}, timed to the dubbed video.

How long does it take?

{% if tgt == "ko" %}A few minutes per video minute on our test GPU; we have not timed it again since the Korean voices changed{% else %}About 2 minutes per video minute on our test GPU (measured on a 2.2-minute clip, November 2026){% endif %}, plus any wait in the queue.
