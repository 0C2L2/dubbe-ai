Questions

Does it keep the original speaker's voice?

Not by default: DUBBE picks a preset voice close to each speaker's pitch. If you confirm a speaker agreed, the editor can clone them from the video for that project. You can also choose another preset, or a clone of your own voice.

Can I upload only audio?

Not yet — upload a video (MP4 or MOV).

Do I get subtitles?

Yes: SRT and WebVTT in {{ a }} and in {{ b }}, timed to the dubbed video.

How long does it take?

On our test GPU {% if tgt == "ko" %}about 77 seconds per video minute (the Korean voice is slower){% else %}about 30 seconds per video minute{% endif %}, plus any wait in the queue.

Can I use the dub commercially?

Not during the beta: the translation model is licensed for non-commercial use only. See the Terms.
