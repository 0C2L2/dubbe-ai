Safety

Words in someone's mouth, with rules

Prevent

  • Every upload needs a confirmation that you own the video or have a licence that allows changing it.
  • Your email must be confirmed before you can dub.
  • Cloning your own voice: a live recording of sentences picked at that moment, checked by speech recognition; no uploads. Shared clones are reviewed before they are listed and can be reported.
  • Cloning a speaker in your video only after you confirm that person agreed; the clone stays inside that video and can never be saved, shared or published. To use a voice elsewhere, its owner clones it and shares it.
  • Every cloning step needs a verified phone, one per account.
{{ icon("cc") }}

Inform

  • Every dubbed file carries an "AI-dubbed with DUBBE" note in its metadata.
  • The download page says the video is AI-generated.
  • Speech made with Chatterbox, the voice of the languages other than English, Korean, Uzbek and Kazakh, also carries its inaudible audio watermark (Resemble AI's Perth). The English, Korean, Uzbek and Kazakh voices (Ming, CosyVoice, Navoiy, Piper) add none: the label is what marks them.
{{ icon("search") }}

Detect

  • We read every misuse report.
  • Sentences spoken in a real person's voice are checked for swear words and scam patterns (links, phone or account numbers, money requests); the team sees what is found. Lies cannot be caught this way: report them.
{{ icon("out") }}

Enforce

  • Dubs that break the Use policy are deleted.
  • Accounts that break it on purpose are closed.
  • A reported account can be frozen: nothing of it can be deleted until the report is resolved. We keep a 90-day record of what is done with real people's voices and give it to the police or a court when the law requires, never to the person who reported.

AI notice

Under Korea's AI Basic Act (in force since 22 January 2026), people should be told when content is generated by AI. Every DUBBE dub is made by AI models: speech recognition, machine translation and synthetic speech. A person may have corrected some sentences, but the voice is always synthetic. Keep the label when you publish a dub, and tell your viewers it is AI-dubbed.
