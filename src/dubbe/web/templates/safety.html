Safety

A dub puts words in someone's mouth in another language. These are the rules and limits we built in — sized to a student project, and stated honestly.

Prevent

  • Every upload needs a confirmation that you own the video or have a licence that allows changing it.
  • Your email must be confirmed before you can dub.
  • Cloning your own voice: a live recording of sentences picked at that moment, checked by speech recognition — no uploads. Shared clones are reviewed before they are listed and can be reported.
  • Cloning a speaker in your video only after you confirm that person agreed; the clone stays inside that project, or private in your voices, and can never be published.

Detect

  • We read every misuse report.
  • We have no automatic detector for misuse; say so if you see it.

Enforce

  • Dubs that break the Use policy are deleted.
  • Accounts that break it on purpose are closed.

Inform

  • Every dubbed file carries an "AI-dubbed with DUBBE" note in its metadata.
  • The download page says the video is AI-generated.
  • Speech made with Chatterbox — every language except English presets, and every voice clone — also carries its inaudible audio watermark (Resemble AI's Perth).

AI notice

Under Korea's AI Basic Act (in force since 22 January 2026), people should be told when content is generated by AI. Every DUBBE dub is made by AI models: speech recognition, machine translation and synthetic speech. A person may have corrected some sentences, but the voice is always synthetic. Keep the label when you publish a dub, and tell your viewers it is AI-dubbed.

Known limits: the translation can be wrong while sounding fluent; review flags do not catch every error; names and technical terms are often mistranslated; the voice follows the original's loudness and pitch but does not reliably carry its emotion; similar voices can be taken for one speaker.

Report misuse

If you find a dub made with DUBBE that impersonates someone, deceives viewers, or uses a video without permission, report it. Include a link and what is wrong. We answer within 2 working days.
